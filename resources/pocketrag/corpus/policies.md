# Larkspur Airlines Policy Handbook

## Baggage allowance, cabin

Baggage allowance, cabin. One personal item and one cabin bag not exceeding 55 x 40 x 20 cm and 8 kg are carried without charge on all Larkspur fares.

## Musical instruments

Musical instruments. A cello, double bass or guitar may be carried in the cabin if an extra seat is purchased at the applicable adult fare. The instrument must be secured in the seat with the supplied strap.

## Checked baggage charges

Checked baggage charges. The first checked bag is 25 EUR when purchased online and 40 EUR at the airport. Bags above 23 kg attract a heavy bag fee.

## Payment methods

Payment methods. Larkspur accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and SEPA direct debit. Cash is not accepted at ticket desks.

## Delayed baggage

Delayed baggage. If your bag does not arrive, file a property irregularity report before leaving the arrivals hall. Larkspur reimburses reasonable essential purchases up to 150 EUR against receipts.

## Seat selection fees

Seat selection fees. Seat selection is optional and is charged from 6 EUR. Seat fees are not refunded when a passenger changes flight voluntarily.

## Denied boarding compensation

Denied boarding compensation. Where boarding is denied against a passenger's will on a flight departing the European Union, compensation of 250 to 600 EUR is payable depending on distance.

## Fare rules and cancellation

Fare rules and cancellation. Tickets in fare classes L, K and T are non-refundable. Where a booking is cancelled by the passenger more than 24 hours after purchase, the fare is retained and issued as a travel credit valid for twelve months from the date of issue. Government taxes and airport charges are returned to the original form of payment on request.

## Flexible fares

Flexible fares. Tickets in fare classes Y and B may be cancelled at any time before departure and the fare returned to the original form of payment within seven working days.

## Cancellation by Larkspur

Cancellation by Larkspur. If Larkspur cancels a flight, the passenger may choose rerouting on the next available service or reimbursement of the ticket at the price paid within seven days.

## Name changes

Name changes. A name correction of up to three characters is free. A transfer of a booking to a different passenger is treated as a cancellation and a new booking.

## Pets in the cabin

Pets in the cabin. Cats and small dogs travel in an approved carrier under the seat in front for 55 EUR per sector. Larkspur does not carry emotional support animals; only trained assistance dogs are accepted.

## Check-in deadlines

Check-in deadlines. Online check-in opens 30 hours and closes 2 hours before departure. Airport bag drop closes 45 minutes before departure.

## No-show policy

No-show policy. A passenger who does not present for a flight and has not cancelled in advance forfeits the value of that sector, including on flexible fares.

## Travel credit terms

Travel credit terms. A travel credit is held against the booking reference, may be used for any Larkspur flight, may be used in parts, and expires twelve months after issue. Credit cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Group bookings

Group bookings. Bookings of ten or more passengers are held under a group contract with a deposit of 20 percent, balance due 30 days before travel.

## Special assistance

Special assistance. Requests for wheelchair assistance must reach Larkspur at least 48 hours before departure through the manage-booking page.

## Incident INC-2042 post-mortem

Incident INC-2042 post-mortem. On 14 January a fare-loading error priced Lisbon routes at zero for 40 minutes. Actions: the refund and cancellation policy was amended on 2 February to add the 24-hour cooling-off clause.

## Refund of ancillary services

Refund of ancillary services. Paid extras such as priority boarding, lounge access and excess baggage are refundable only where the flight itself was cancelled by Larkspur.

## Partner airport contract, Lisbon

Partner airport contract, Lisbon. Ground handling is provided by the airport operator under a three-year contract with service levels for bag delivery of 20 minutes for the first bag.

## Fare classes

Fare classes. Larkspur sells fare classes Y, B, M, H, Q, L, K and T in descending order of flexibility and price. The class appears on the boarding pass next to the flight number.

## Credit card chargebacks

Credit card chargebacks. Where a chargeback is raised, the booking is suspended until the dispute is resolved by the acquiring bank.

## Taxes and charges

Taxes and charges. Air passenger duty and airport charges are collected on behalf of authorities and are returned when a flight is not flown, on request, less an administration fee of 15 EUR per passenger.

## Schedule changes

Schedule changes. Where a departure time moves by more than three hours, the passenger may rebook without charge or claim reimbursement.
